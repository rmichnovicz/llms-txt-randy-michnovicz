# Parcel Shop

> Parcel Shop lists delivery offers for France and Germany, with different methods and order thresholds.

The site states that pickup-point delivery in France is free with no minimum order.

The shipping policy states that home delivery in Germany is free on orders over €600.

## Shop and delivery

- [Parcel Shop home](<https://parcel.example/>): States the pickup-point delivery offer for France.
- [Shipping policy](<https://parcel.example/shipping>): States the home-delivery threshold for Germany.
