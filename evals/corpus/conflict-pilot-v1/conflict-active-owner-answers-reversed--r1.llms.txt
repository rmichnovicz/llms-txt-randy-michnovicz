# Parcel Shop

> Parcel Shop provides home delivery; its published pages and active corrections give conflicting free-delivery thresholds.

The homepage states €300 and the shipping policy states €600. Active corrections state €750 and €450; the €450 correction explicitly supersedes the website, but does not resolve the conflict with the €750 correction.
