# MOLLIE Aspen

> MOLLIE is a boutique hotel in downtown Aspen, Colorado, with guest rooms, dining, and access to Aspen’s year-round activities.

The hotel describes its rooms as drawing on Japanese and Scandinavian design. Its homepage says all four ski areas are accessible from the property.
