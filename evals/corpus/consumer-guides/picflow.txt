# Picflow

> Picflow provides client galleries for sharing media and collecting feedback and approvals from creative teams and clients.

Picflow supports common image formats, RAW files from major camera brands, video, and audio; it converts RAW files into previews.

The pricing FAQ says the free plan has no time limit, paid plans offer a seven-day trial, and reviewers do not need a user license.
