# Neon Electrical — Auckland Electrician

> Neon Electrical provides electrical work for Auckland homeowners and businesses, including repairs, safety upgrades and renovations.

Based in Ōtāhuhu, the business lists service areas across South, East, Central and West Auckland and the North Shore.

Neon Electrical says its electrician is a registered member of Master Electricians New Zealand. It reports a $20,000 workmanship guarantee for residential jobs and $5 million in public liability insurance.
