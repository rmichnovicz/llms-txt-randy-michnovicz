# WE IN STYLE

> WE IN STYLE describes itself as a designer-fashion store for men, women, and children, offering clothing, shoes, and accessories.

The homepage and shipping policy give different free-home-delivery thresholds \(€600 and €300, respectively\). Check the shipping policy and checkout for applicable charges.

Returns must be requested within 30 days of receipt. The refund policy says exchanges are unavailable and customers pay return shipping and customs fees.
