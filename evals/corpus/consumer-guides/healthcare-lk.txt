# DocPP \| HealthCare.LK

> DocPP is a cloud-based clinic management system presented for doctors, dispensers, and nurses in Sri Lanka.

The site offers a 14-day free trial and describes standard and custom plans. Its reported subscription prices conflict, so this guide does not quote a price.

## Product and plans

- [DocPP overview](<https://healthcare.lk/>): Introduces clinic features, intended users, plan types, and a recorded demonstration from 2022.

## Try DocPP
