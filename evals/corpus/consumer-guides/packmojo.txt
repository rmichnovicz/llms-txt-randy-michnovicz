# PackMojo

> PackMojo offers custom packaging for brands, including mailer boxes, with options to configure packaging, order samples, and arrange production and shipping.

PackMojo lists a 100-unit minimum for custom mailer boxes, applied separately to each variation in size, material, or artwork.

PackMojo says custom samples are reimbursable with an order of at least 500 units.

PackMojo reports typical production lead times of 12–16 days; larger bulk orders may take 3–4 weeks, and shipping time varies by method.
