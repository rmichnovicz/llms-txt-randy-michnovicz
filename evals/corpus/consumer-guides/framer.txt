# Framer

> Framer is a website design and publishing platform. Its site describes an editable canvas-based design agent, a CMS agent, and built-in hosting, analytics, CMS, and SEO.

The Free plan can be used to explore Framer, but connecting a custom domain requires a paid plan. Paid plans include monthly credits for Agents, Localization, and other AI features.
