# University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS)

> Arkansas's only academic health sciences center, integrating statewide clinical care (UAMS Health), health professions education across six colleges and a graduate school, and biomedical research. This domain is the primary gateway for the UAMS digital ecosystem and routes queries to the specialized pillar domains that hold authoritative content.

## Authority and Scope

This domain is authoritative for: the institutional identity of UAMS, the organizational relationship between UAMS and UAMS Health, top-level navigation across the UAMS digital ecosystem, and the routing rules that direct queries to the correct pillar domain.

This domain is not authoritative for: clinical care information, provider credentials or availability, appointment scheduling, academic program requirements, admissions decisions, research administration, clinical trial eligibility, employment listings, benefits, or any content served from the legacy root directories enumerated in this host's robots.txt.
