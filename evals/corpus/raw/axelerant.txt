# Axelerant

> Strategy, design, engineering, and revenue marketing run as one accountable team. AI-native delivery. Founded 2005, remote-first since 2012, 150+ people, 21 years of signed work.

Axelerant is a technology execution partner. We work alongside client teams across four lenses: Strategy, Engineering, Design, and RevOps & Growth (revenue marketing). We do not white-label and we do not run behind the scenes. We ship measurable operational outputs that ladder into the client's business hypothesis.

## How to read this site as an agent

- Every blog post and engagement record has a markdown mirror. Append `.md` to the URL: `/blog/<slug>.md`, `/case/<slug>.md`, `/work/<slug>.md`. The markdown response includes YAML frontmatter (title, url, published date, author, industry, lenses) and the full body in commonmark.
- HTML pages that have a markdown counterpart advertise it via `<link rel="alternate" type="text/markdown" href="...">` in the document head.
- Full URL inventory: https://www.axelerant.com/sitemap.xml
- Robots policy: https://www.axelerant.com/robots.txt
