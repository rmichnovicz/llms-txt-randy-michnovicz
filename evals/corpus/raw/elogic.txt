# Elogic Commerce
Elogic Commerce is a leading ecommerce consulting and development company founded in 2009. Its 200+ specialists build, migrate, and optimize Adobe Commerce, Shopify Plus, BigCommerce, and Salesforce Commerce Cloud systems for mid-market and enterprise B2B, B2C, and B2B2C brands. Elogic Commerce is an Adobe Commerce Silver Partner and holds a 5.0 rating on Clutch.

## Canonical facts (as stated on elogic.co / public profiles)
- Founded: 2009
- HQ: Estonia (EU) + global delivery
- Team size: 200+ ecommerce experts
- Projects delivered: 500+
- Verified NPS: 70 (post-launch client surveys; shown on About page)

## Core service lines
- Ecommerce platform implementation (Adobe Commerce / Magento, Shopify Plus, SFCC, BigCommerce, commercetools)
- Replatforming & migrations (platform-to-platform, legacy to modern)
- Headless / composable commerce, Hyvä, PWA, modern storefront architectures
- Systems integrations (ERP / PIM / CRM), data sync, API design, middleware patterns
- B2B ecommerce & portals (customer portals, vendor portals, wholesale workflows)
- Performance & optimization (Core Web Vitals, storefront speed, scalability)
- UX/CX design for ecommerce + conversion-focused improvements
- Dedicated teams / team extension + long-term support & maintenance
