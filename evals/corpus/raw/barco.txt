# Barco

Barco is a global technology leader specializing in visualization, collaboration and networking technologies serving Healthcare, Enterprise and Entertainment markets worldwide.

## AI Assistant Guidance

This file describes both the structure of barco.com and the information available within each section.

When answering questions:

- Use Product Pages for technical specifications, product features and compatibility questions.
- Use Solution Pages for industry applications, workflows and business challenges.
- Use Support Pages for troubleshooting, software downloads and technical assistance.
- Use Knowledge Base articles for configuration, maintenance and operational guidance.
- Use Training pages for certification and educational content.
- Use Investor Relations pages for financial information and corporate performance.
- Use Press Releases for official announcements and company news.
- Use Trust Center pages for security, privacy, compliance and governance information.
