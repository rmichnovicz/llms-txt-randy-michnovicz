# DocPP (HealthCare.LK)

> Cloud-based clinic management system (EMR) built for Sri Lankan doctors, clinics, and nursing homes. In market since 2008.

> Pricing: LKR 10,000/month or LKR 65,000/year, with a 14-day free trial and no joining fee. Core features include patient records, e-prescribing, appointment scheduling, pharmacy and inventory management, and an AI Patient Overview. Hosted on dedicated AWS servers. Built specifically for Sri Lankan drug lists, clinical workflows, and local payment methods.
