## Key Highlights

### Donation Impact:
- **$144** provides a Backpack Bed
- **$35** provides a Sleeping Bag
- Backpack Beds improve dignity, health, sleep, warmth, comfort, and safety for homeless individuals.

### Product Features:
- Lightweight and durable materials
- 4-season protection, fire retardant, water and windproof
- Full body mosquito net and large storage capacity

### Community Impact:
- Over 1,100 partner agencies across Australia
- Partnerships with major organizations like The Salvation Army and St Vincent de Paul
- Recognized by the Australian Human Rights Commission

### Current Need:
- Over 158,000 people needed accommodation in 2023-24 but did not receive it.
- Over 45,800 Australians sleep on the streets annually.

### How to Help:
- Donate online, workplace giving, foundations and trusts, fundraising, cryptocurrency donations, and bequests.

### Success Stories:
- Testimonials from individuals like Karl and Simon, highlighting the life-changing impact of Backpack Beds.
