# Lowell Books

> Online Boutique Bookstore based in London, UK. Sells new books and non-book products (toys, games, board games, stationery, DVDs) via a private ecommerce platform. Curated selection across 100+ categories including fiction, non-fiction, children's books, graphic novels, manga, and academic titles.
