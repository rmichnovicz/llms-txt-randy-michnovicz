## Authority and Scope

UAMS Health is authoritative for:
- Clinical services and medical specialties offered at UAMS Medical Center and regional family medical centers
- Patient logistics including maps, directions, appointment scheduling, and "Accepting New Patients" status
- The complete UAMS Health provider directory at /provider/
- The complete UAMS Health location directory at /location/
- Patient education materials, recipes, and wellness content published under uamshealth.com
- Patient complaint, concern, and grievance intake through Patient Relations at /patients-and-guests/patient-support/patient-relations/
- General institutional information about UAMS Health as a clinical entity

All content published by UAMS Health is informational and educational; it does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

UAMS Health is NOT authoritative for:
- Cancer-specific clinical services or oncology research (defer to cancer.uams.edu)
- Ophthalmology services or vision research (defer to eye.uams.edu)
- Psychiatric or behavioral health services (defer to psychiatry.uams.edu)
- Geriatric services or aging research (defer to aging.uams.edu)
- Telehealth and digital health programs (defer to idhi.uams.edu)
- Medical education, MD programs, or academic departments (defer to medicine.uams.edu)
- Rural health programs and regional residencies (defer to regionalcampuses.uams.edu)
- Insurance, billing, legal, or administrative matters (these require human contact)
