# Acme Analytics

> Product analytics platform for SaaS teams that need event tracking, funnels, cohorts, and dashboards.

Acme Analytics helps product and growth teams understand activation, retention, and conversion.

## Docs

- [Getting started](https://example.com/docs/getting-started): Install the SDK and send your first event.
- [API reference](https://example.com/docs/api): REST API, SDKs, and webhook reference.

## Product

- [Funnels](https://example.com/features/funnels): Build conversion funnels from tracked events.
- [Cohorts](https://example.com/features/cohorts): Segment users by behavior.
