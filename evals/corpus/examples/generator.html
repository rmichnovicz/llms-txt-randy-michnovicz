Frequently asked questions

What should an llms.txt example include?

A useful example includes an H1 title, a short blockquote summary, optional context, and grouped markdown links to the most important pages.

Can I copy an llms.txt template?

Yes. Start from a template, then replace the title, summary, sections, URLs, and notes with real pages from your own site.
